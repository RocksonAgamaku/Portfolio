04 / STUDENT LEADERSHIP

College of Science · 2024/2025

Leadership at KNUST

Serving students. Building opportunities.

Elected Financial Secretary of the College of Science (2024/2025), after previously serving unopposed for the Computer Science Society. Below: the handover from the outgoing executive, the swearing-in, and the wider team.

Election journey

The Financial Secretary campaign was contested — I placed third in the vetting round, then won the general election outright.

Student support & technical learning

Beyond elected office: distributing food supplies to vulnerable students through the Food Bank Initiative, and presenting after AWS cloud training at Amalitech.

Women in science

With faculty at the Women in Science program, where I helped host trailblazing women in STEM including Prof. Elsie Effah Kaufman (University of Ghana) and Prof. Linda Aurelia Ofori (KNUST).

Career development

Volunteering with Kissy's Connect to organize the Career Development Summit III, held at the College of Science Auditorium (Allotey Auditorium), KNUST.
